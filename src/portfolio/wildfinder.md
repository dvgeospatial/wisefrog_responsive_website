      picture of local biodiversity. By turning abstract species lists into
      concrete geographical data, it serves as an essential bridge between
      public curiosity and grassroots conservation.
    heading: Promote citizen science with wildFinder
  - type: text
    body: The application makes biodiversity immediate and tangible for the public
      by showing exactly which species have been spotted near local footpaths
      and access lands. This encourages purposeful outdoor recreation, turning a
      casual walk into an interactive wildlife hunt. Seeing their own
      iNaturalist discoveries appear on a live public map creates a highly
      rewarding feedback loop for users, lowering the barrier to entry and
      motivating them to keep recording their findings.
