      promote engagement in citizen science by presenting wildlife sightings on
      a map providing a clear picture of local biodiversity. Users of wildFinder
      can easily review details of individual biodiversity recorded in their
      local area and be inspired to start recording their own sightings using
      the iNaturalistUK app. Additional mapping layers show publicly accessible
      sites and walking routes.  The iNaturalist is is one of the worlds most
      popular nature apps. iNaturalist is promoted by the National Biodiversity
      Network (NBN) and it is entirely free to use. By framing biodiversity
      observation data for a defined local area in an easy and engaging app
      wildFinder helps to promote real engagement and meaningful citizen science
      activity .
